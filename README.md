## Teaching approach

The review connects related statements, separates definitions from computational methods, and highlights when a proof requires additional care. The comparison sheet helps organize revision; historical topic frequency is not a prediction or guarantee of future exam content.

## Learning goals

- Explain the relationships among rank, invertibility, eigenvalues and linear systems.
- Recognize assumptions in proof questions and choose an appropriate argument.
- Connect projections and orthogonality to least-squares problems.

The complete tutorial question sheets and their worked solutions are included above. Original past-exam collections and third-party reference packs are kept separately. Course and past-exam topic references remain the work of their original authors; inclusion of a problem does not claim original authorship of the underlying question.
